Would You Know What to Do?

2 minutes to learn skills that could save a life

In a drowning emergency, brain damage begins in 4-6 minutes.
Emergency services take 7-10 minutes to arrive.

You are your child's first responder.

If You Find a Child Unresponsive in Water

1

Get Them Out

Remove them from the water immediately. Support their head and neck.
2

Check for Response

Tap their shoulders firmly. Shout their name. Look for breathing (chest rising).
3

Call 911

If unresponsive, call 911 immediately (or have someone call while you start CPR).
4

Start CPR

See instructions below for children and infants.

Children (1-8 years)

  1. Heel of one hand on center of chest
  2. Push hard & fast — 2 inches deep
  3. 100-120 pushes per minute
  4. After 30, give 2 breaths
  5. Repeat until help arrives

Infants (under 1 year)

  1. 2 fingers on center of chest
  2. Push 1.5 inches deep
  3. 100-120 per minute
  4. Cover mouth AND nose for breaths
  5. 30 compressions, 2 breaths

The Beat to Remember

Push to "Stayin' Alive" by the Bee Gees
It's exactly 100 beats per minute

Take a CPR Class

American Heart Association — cpr.heart.org
