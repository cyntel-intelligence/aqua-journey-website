Surprising Sources of Danger

🛁 Bathtubs
#1 location for infant drowning
🪣 Buckets & containers
Top-heavy toddlers tip in and can't get out
🚽 Toilets
Toddlers can fall in headfirst and get stuck
🐕 Pet bowls, coolers, fountains
Any standing water is a risk

Room-by-Room Safety Checklist

🚿 Bathroom

☐ Never leave children under 5 alone in the bath
☐ Drain the tub immediately after use
☐ Install toilet seat locks
☐ Keep bathroom doors closed

🌳 Backyard

☐ Empty kiddie pools after every use
☐ Fence pools on all 4 sides with self-latching gates
☐ Remove above-ground pool ladders when not in use
☐ Cover hot tubs with locked, rigid covers

🏠 Around the House

☐ Empty mop buckets immediately
☐ Store buckets upside down or out of reach
☐ Don't leave coolers with melted ice accessible
☐ Be aware of neighbors' pools
