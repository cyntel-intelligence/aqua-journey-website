Summer Is Coming

Is Your Child Ready?

As the weather warms up, pool parties, beach vacations, and lake trips are on the horizon. Now is the time to prepare.

Pre-Summer Swim Checklist

👶 For Your Child

☐ Refresh swim skills (even strong swimmers get rusty)
☐ Review water safety rules together
☐ Practice what to do if they fall in unexpectedly
☐ Make sure swimsuits are bright colors (remember email #1!)

👨‍👩‍👧 For You

☐ Review CPR basics (take a refresher course if needed)
☐ Check that pool barriers and alarms are working
☐ Stock up on sunscreen and swim gear
☐ Plan the "Water Watcher" system for gatherings

Vacation Water Safety

Traveling somewhere with water? Keep these tips in mind:

🏖️ Beach

  • Swim near lifeguards
  • Learn the flag warning system (red = no swimming)
  • Watch for rip currents — swim parallel to shore to escape

⛵ Boats

  • Coast Guard-approved life jackets for all children
  • Life jackets ON at all times, not just "nearby"
  • No swimming off boats without adult in water

🏨 Hotels

  • Never assume pools have lifeguards — most don't
  • Check fence/gate security
  • Supervision rules apply everywhere
