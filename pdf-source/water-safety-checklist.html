Water Safety Checklist for Parents

Print this checklist and keep it near your pool

1. Pool & Water Area Safety

  • 4-sided fence with self-latching gate
  • No direct access from house to pool
  • Pool alarm installed
  • Drain covers secure and compliant
  • Safety equipment nearby (ring, pole)

2. Supervision Rules

  • Designated "Water Watcher" at all times
  • No phones/distractions while watching
  • Arm's reach for non-swimmers
  • 10/20 rule: scan every 10s, reach in 20s

3. Emergency Preparedness

  • CPR certified (both parents/caregivers)
  • Phone accessible poolside
  • First aid kit nearby
  • Address posted for 911

4. Child Safety Skills

  • Child enrolled in swim lessons
  • Child knows: never swim alone
  • Child knows: ask before entering water
  • High-visibility swimwear
Teach them to swim — it's the best protection
